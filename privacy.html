Privacy Policy

My Clipper runs entirely on its owner's computer. It has no server and collects no data from anyone else.

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the tool receives the account's basic profile information (open id, display name, avatar) and an access token from TikTok. These are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos as drafts. Nothing is shared with third parties.

The owner can disconnect at any time by removing the app under TikTok's Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions, and by deleting the token file on their computer.
